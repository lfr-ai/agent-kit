# Code of conduct

## Standards

Be respectful and constructive across project discussions, reviews, issues,
and spaces where someone represents the project. Welcome different experience
levels and backgrounds; focus criticism on technical work, not people.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual
advances, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and disclosing another
person's private information without consent.

## Reporting and privacy

Report privately through `conduct_contact` in
[maintainers.json](.github/maintainers.json); never post sensitive evidence
in a public issue. The configured contact is
[lfr@tik-ai.dk](mailto:lfr@tik-ai.dk), matching the owner's existing conduct policy.
Reporters may request confidentiality; maintainers share evidence only as needed
to investigate fairly or meet legal obligations.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or limit participation while investigating.
Responses should be proportionate: clarification or warning, temporary
restriction, then exclusion for severe or repeated misconduct.
Consider context, impact, recurrence, and safety; communicate the decision and
its scope privately where practical.

A person involved in a complaint must not be its sole decision-maker.
Request reconsideration through the same private route and identify uninvolved
maintainers where possible. If none exist, record that independence is limited.

These expectations apply consistently to maintainers and contributors.
