# Support

## Questions and defects

- Read [README.md](README.md), [compatibility](docs/compatibility.md), and the
  [policy contract](docs/policy.md) first.
- Use the repository's issue forms for a minimal synthetic defect or feature proposal.
- Include toolkit/APM/Python/platform versions, expected behavior, and the failing command.
- Do not upload company repository contents, credentials, personal data, or private logs.

This initial toolkit is community-supported with no response-time or uptime guarantee.
Native IDE/cloud sessions remain uncertified unless explicit versioned evidence says otherwise.

## Private reports

Do not report vulnerabilities or conduct incidents in public issues.
Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Private reporting contacts are configured in the maintainer metadata; hosted
GitHub reporting still requires an administrator to enable it after publication.
